Wallpapers of the dual-screen build's bottom screen ([Dual Screen] Background = Wallpaper).

Choose one in the settings menu (Display, Dual Screen, Bottom screen wallpaper), or Change every 30 seconds to show each
in turn. Any 24-bit BMP put in this folder is listed there (up to 16 are cycled). It is scaled to cover the screen.

mhxx_blue.bmp: an MHXX picture (blue), published online.
mhxx_title_down.bmp: MHXX's title bottom screen, arc/title.arc, GUI\99_texture\title_down_BM_NOMIP.
mhxx_reward_back.bmp: MHXX's reward screen background, arc/result.arc, GUI\99_texture\rewardback_BM_NOMIP.
mhgu_title_down.bmp: MHGU's title bottom picture (Switch), eng/arc/title_eng.arc, GUI\99_texture\HD_title_down_BM_NOMIP.
